What journeys does ArcTerra Expeditions offer?

ArcTerra runs ARCX ocean voyages (Banda Islands, Komodo, a Banda Sea dive liveaboard and a Mentawai surf expedition, 6 to 8 days), TERRA Y land expeditions (Tibet to Everest, 11 days; Tanzania and Kenya, 14 days), Polar Crossings voyages to Antarctica (8 days) and South Georgia (17 days), and eclipse journeys to Egypt, Komodo and the Amazon in 2027 and 2028.
