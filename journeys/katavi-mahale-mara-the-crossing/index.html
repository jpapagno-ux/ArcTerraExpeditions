Fourteen days from Tanzania's wild west to Kenya's great river. Katavi's buffalo plains and hippo pools, the chimpanzees of the Mahale forest on the shore of Lake Tanganyika, then the Mara River in crossing season with Alex Walker's Serian. At a glance

The short answers

What is The Crossing?

A 14-day private safari across Tanzania and Kenya: Katavi National Park, the chimpanzees of Mahale on Lake Tanganyika, then the Masai Mara during the Great Migration river crossings.

Who is it for?

Travellers who have done a first safari and want remote parks, walking, a chimpanzee trek and the migration, with no more than six guests.

What are the highlights?

Walking Katavi's floodplain, a fly-camp night, one hour with wild chimpanzees, a wooden dhow on Lake Tanganyika, the Mara River crossings, a dawn balloon and a night drive.

How active is it?

Moderate. The chimpanzee trek can be steep and hot; walks are at a gentle pace. There are several light-aircraft flights with soft-bag luggage limits.

Travel information

Before you go
Visas & entry

Tanzania issues visas online (eVisa); apply at least a few weeks ahead. Kenya requires an Electronic Travel Authorisation for most visitors, applied for online before travel. Passports need six months' validity and blank pages. Check the current rules for your nationality before booking.

Health

Katavi, Mahale and the Mara are malarial areas; speak to a travel clinic about antimalarials and vaccinations. Carry your yellow fever certificate if you have one and check whether your route requires it. Chimp trekking requires guests to be healthy on the day; anyone with a cold stays behind to protect the chimpanzees, and masks are worn near them.

Flights & luggage

Fly into Kilimanjaro (JRO) the day before the safari, and out of Nairobi (NBO) after 15:00 on the last day. Light aircraft carry soft bags only, usually 15 kg including hand luggage. The flights to and from Mahale are long, with refuelling stops.

Weather in August

Dry season across the route. Katavi and the Mara are cool at dawn, around 10–15 °C, and warm by day, around 25–28 °C. Mahale is warm and humid in the forest; Lake Tanganyika is warm for swimming at the camp beach.

What to pack

Neutral colours (khaki, olive, sand). Avoid dark blue and black in Katavi, where they attract tsetse flies. A warm layer for early drives and the balloon, sturdy shoes for the chimp trek, a swimsuit, binoculars, a hat and sunscreen.

Money & connectivity

US dollars are widely used for tips; bring clean notes printed after 2013. Wi-Fi is limited or absent at Chada and Greystoke; the Serian camps have connectivity in the main areas.

Questions travellers ask

FAQ
When is the best time for the Mara River crossings?

Crossings usually peak from August to October, when the herds move between the Serengeti and the Masai Mara. They depend on rain and the herds and are never guaranteed, which is why this route spends three nights beside the river.

How do I get to Katavi and Mahale?

By light aircraft from Arusha, refuelling at Tabora. Mahale has no roads; the last stretch to Greystoke Mahale is about 90 minutes by wooden dhow along Lake Tanganyika.

How hard is the chimpanzee trek?

It varies by day: trackers find the chimpanzees first, and the walk can take from a few minutes to several hours uphill on forest trails. Time with the chimpanzees is limited to one hour, and guests must be 12 or older and healthy.

Is there a luggage limit?

Yes. Light aircraft usually allow about 15 kg per person in soft bags, including hand luggage. We confirm the limit with your documents.

Do I need visas for Tanzania and Kenya?

Most visitors need an e-visa or electronic travel authorisation for both countries. Apply online before travel and carry printed copies.

Is it suitable for children?

Children aged 12 and over are welcome; the Mahale chimpanzee trek has a minimum age of 12.
