The short answer

What is ARC | TERRA XY?

ARC | TERRA XY is a luxury exploration house with two expedition brands. ARCX runs private ocean voyages aboard traditional and expedition yachts; TERRA Y runs overland expeditions by 4×4, bush plane, rail and on foot. Every journey is planned as a coordinate ledger, day by day and place by place.

ARCX · by sea

Small-ship voyages through remote archipelagos, with diving, snorkelling and island landings. At launch: the Banda Islands (Nutmeg & Fire) and Komodo National Park (Dragons & Corals), aboard the phinisi Dunia Baru.

TERRA Y · by land

Overland expeditions to high plateaus and wild parks, paced for the terrain. At launch: Tibet and the North Face of Everest (Thin Air) and Katavi, Mahale and the Masai Mara (The Crossing).

Questions travellers ask

FAQ

What is the difference between ARCX and TERRA Y?

ARCX is the ocean brand: voyages by yacht through remote seas and islands. TERRA Y is the land brand: overland expeditions by vehicle, light aircraft, rail and on foot. Both share the same planning ledger and standards.

Where do ARCX voyages go?

The launch voyages sail Indonesia: the Banda Islands from Ambon (8 days) and Komodo National Park from Labuan Bajo (6 days), aboard the phinisi Dunia Baru.

Where do TERRA Y expeditions go?

The launch expeditions cross Tibet to the North Face of Everest (11 days, Chengdu to Kathmandu) and East Africa from Katavi and Mahale in Tanzania to the Masai Mara in Kenya (14 days).

Are the journeys private?

Most are private: the ARCX voyages charter the whole yacht, and The Crossing runs for 2 to 6 guests. Thin Air also runs as a small group of up to 16 guests on set dates.

How do I book?

Open a journey and send an enquiry. Dates and prices are confirmed for each departure, and we hold places while the details are agreed.
