SYZYGY 01 · SZ·01/27 · Total solar eclipse

The Long Noon: the 2027 total solar eclipse at Luxor

On Monday 2 August 2027 the Moon’s shadow crosses Egypt and puts out the Sun over Luxor for a little over six minutes around 13:05 local time, one of the longest totalities of the century. ARCX UMBRA comes down the Red Sea by private yacht and crosses the desert by 4×4; TERRA Y TOTALITY flies to Aswan and sails north by dahabiya. They meet in Luxor the evening before.

SYZYGY 03 · SZ·03/28 · Annular solar eclipse

The Ring over the Drowned Forest: the 2028 annular eclipse in the Amazon

On Wednesday 26 January 2028 the Moon passes centrally in front of the Sun over the Amazon but is too far from Earth to cover it, leaving a ring of fire for about ten minutes, close to the longest annularity of the century. The centre line crosses the Balbina reservoir, a drowned forest of thousands of islands two hours north of Manaus. ARCX UMBRA comes by riverboat through the Rio Negro; TERRA Y TOTALITY drives north into the waterfall country. Both arrive at the reservoir the evening before.

  • Plan BRun up to 40 km across the reservoir to a clearer patch, decided at 08:00 from satellite images and local radar.
  • Plan CAn eclipse flight above the clouds: a charter aircraft on standby at Manaus (about 2 h by road) for a group that chooses it in advance (TBC).
  • HonestyThis is an Amazon expedition with a chance at the ring, not a clear-sky bet. The forest, the rivers and the people are the trip; the ring is the prize.

Eclipse questions

When is the 2027 total solar eclipse in Egypt?

Monday 2 August 2027. At Luxor totality comes around 13:05 local time (about 10:05 UT) and lasts a little over six minutes. Greatest eclipse is at 10:07:50 UT about 60 km south-east of Luxor, with 6 minutes 23 seconds of totality.

How do the two Egypt lines travel?

ARCX UMBRA sails from Hurghada down the Red Sea by private yacht to Quseir, then crosses the Eastern Desert by 4×4 on the ancient Wadi Hammamat road to Luxor. TERRA Y TOTALITY flies from Cairo to Aswan and sails north to Luxor by dahabiya, a traditional Nile sailing boat.

When is the annular eclipse in Brazil?

Wednesday 26 January 2028. On the Balbina reservoir north of Manaus the ring of fire lasts about ten minutes, around 10:27 to 10:37 local time (about 14:27 to 14:37 UT). The longest annularity anywhere, 10 minutes 31 seconds, is in Pará.

What is the difference between a total and an annular eclipse?

In a total eclipse the Moon covers the whole Sun and the sky goes dark enough to see the corona. In an annular eclipse the Moon is farther from Earth, looks slightly smaller and leaves a bright ring of the Sun around it. The light dims but it never gets dark.

Is it safe to look at an annular eclipse?

Never without protection. Certified ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses or solar filters must stay on for the whole annular eclipse, including the ring. Only during the brief totality of a total eclipse can filters come off. Glasses and filtered binoculars are supplied.

What are the weather odds in the Amazon in January?

January is the rainy season and average cloud along the track is about 80–90%. Big water such as the Balbina reservoir reduces cloud a little, so the plan is to watch from boats that can move to gaps, with a reserved eclipse flight above the clouds as an option.

How far ahead should I book?

Luxor accommodation in the path and private boats in both regions are limited. Register interest now; departures open with a deposit once vessels, dates and prices are confirmed.
