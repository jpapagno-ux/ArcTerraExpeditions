Syzygy is the moment three bodies stand in a straight line. On each SYZYGY journey two lines travel separately, one by sea and one by land, and meet on a single coordinate for the eclipse. Where others go, we don’t: one way in, one way out, one minute in the shadow.

X · ARCX UMBRA

The sea line. Private yacht or phinisi, reefs and anchorages, arriving on the coordinate from the water.

Y · TERRA Y TOTALITY

The land line. River, road and village, arriving on the coordinate overland.

X × Y · The eclipse

The meridian (X) and the parallel (Y) of the viewing site cross where the Sun, Moon and Earth line up.

SYZYGY 01 · SZ·01/27 · Total solar eclipse

The Long Noon: the 2027 total solar eclipse at Luxor

On Monday 2 August 2027 the Moon’s shadow crosses Egypt and puts out the Sun over Luxor for a little over six minutes around 13:05 local time, one of the longest totalities of the century. Two ARC | TERRA XY expeditions converge on one coordinate in the path: ARCX UMBRA by private yacht down the Red Sea, and TERRA Y TOTALITY by dahabiya on the Nile. They meet in Luxor the evening before.

SYZYGY 02 · SZ·02/28 · Total lunar eclipse

The Copper Midnight: a New Year blood moon over Komodo

On the night of 31 December 2028 the full Moon passes through the Earth’s shadow. In Indonesia totality runs from 00:16 to 01:28 on New Year’s morning (WITA), 72 minutes of copper Moon high over Komodo. ARCX UMBRA sails the park by phinisi while TERRA Y TOTALITY crosses Flores overland; the two lines meet in Labuan Bajo and watch from one anchorage below Padar.

Eclipse questions

When is the 2027 total solar eclipse in Egypt?

Monday 2 August 2027. At Luxor totality comes around 13:05 local time (about 10:05 UT) and lasts a little over six minutes. Greatest eclipse is at 10:07:50 UT about 60 km south-east of Luxor, with 6 minutes 23 seconds of totality.

Why Luxor for the 2027 eclipse?

Luxor sits almost on the centre line, so totality there is close to the maximum. The Sun is more than 80° high, and August skies in Upper Egypt are among the clearest of any eclipse site.

What is the difference between ARCX UMBRA and TERRA Y TOTALITY?

They are the two lines of each SYZYGY journey. ARCX UMBRA travels by sea (a Red Sea yacht in 2027, a Komodo phinisi in 2028). TERRA Y TOTALITY travels by land (the Nile by dahabiya, Flores overland). Both arrive at the same viewing coordinate for the eclipse.

When is the total lunar eclipse of 31 December 2028?

Totality runs from 16:16 to 17:28 UT, 72 minutes, with greatest eclipse at 16:52 UT. In central Indonesia (WITA, UTC+8) that is 00:16 to 01:28 on 1 January 2029. It is visible from Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia and the Pacific.

Why watch the 2028 blood moon from Komodo?

From Komodo the totally eclipsed Moon stands about 55° high in the north-north-west at midnight on New Year’s Eve, over a dark anchorage with little light pollution.

Do I need eye protection?

For the solar eclipse, yes: certified ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses for every partial phase, removed only during totality. Glasses are supplied. A lunar eclipse is safe to watch with the naked eye.
